How TransitSync Works

Unlike traditional portals that only list direct trains by departure date, TransitSync solves the reverse route: you specify your target arrival deadline and layover tolerance. TransitSync computes direct trains and multi-leg connecting routes ($A \to C \to B$) with intermediate transfer safety and combined fares.
